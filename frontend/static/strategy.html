1. Know which side you are on1. 先弄清楚你在哪一边

The two teams are not playing the same game. The defending team (the declarer and their partner) wins by denying points. The attacking team wins only by collecting them. Every decision you make should follow from which of those two jobs is yours. 两队打的其实不是同一个游戏。防守方（庄家及其对家）靠拦截分数取胜，进攻方只能靠收集分数取胜。你的每一个决定，都应当从“我这一方的任务是什么”出发。

Only 5s (5 points), 10s (10 points) and Ks (10 points) are worth anything. In the standard four-player, two-deck game that is 200 points on the table, and the attackers take over the declaring seat at 80. 只有 5（5 分）、10（10 分）和 K（10 分）算分。在标准的四人两副牌局中，全场共 200 分，进攻方拿到 80 分即可夺庄。

Rule of thumb 要点

Attackers: a trick with no points in it barely matters — chase the tricks that hold 5s, 10s and Ks. Defenders: your goal is not to win tricks, it is to make sure the tricks the attackers win are empty. 进攻方：没有分的一轮几乎无关紧要——要去抢带 5、10、K 的那些轮。防守方：你的目标不是多赢轮次，而是让对手赢到的每一轮都是空的。

2. Count your hand before you do anything2. 出牌之前先数牌

Three numbers decide how your whole hand should be played: 三个数字决定你这一手该怎么打：

  • Trump count. In the four-player, two-deck game an average hand holds about 8–10 trump. Twelve or more and you can control the hand; six or fewer and you should be playing defensively and dumping points. 主牌张数。在四人两副牌的牌局里，平均一手大约有 8–10 张主牌。12 张以上，你可以主导这一局；6 张以下，就该转为防守、专心处理分牌。
  • Tops. Jokers and rank cards. These are your last-trick insurance — count them and do not spend them casually. 大牌。王和级牌。它们是你保最后一轮的保险——先数清楚，别随手打掉。
  • Boss cards. Cards nothing left in the deck can beat: side-suit aces, and any card whose superiors have all been played. These are free tricks — but only while they last. 最大牌。场上已无牌能压过它的牌：副牌的 A，以及所有比它大的牌都已出完的牌。这些是白拿的轮次——但只在它们还“最大”的时候有效。

3. Declare trump on pairs, not just length3. 亮主看对子，不只看张数

Everyone knows to declare the suit they hold the most of. The part most players get wrong is that a pair in the trump suit is worth roughly three to four loose trump cards. Two consecutive trump pairs — a tractor in trump — is better still. Pairs win tricks that singles cannot, and they are what lets you beat an opponent's pair-led trick. 谁都知道要亮自己张数最多的那门花色。多数人搞错的是：主牌花色里的一个对子，价值大约相当于 三到四张散主牌；两个连续的主牌对子（主牌里的拖拉机）更强。对子能赢下散牌赢不了的轮次，也是你压住对手对牌的唯一手段。

Rule of thumb 要点

Do not declare the moment you legally can. Early in the deal you have seen only a fraction of your cards, and a trump suit you commit to at card six can easily finish short. Also: aces and kings play better as side-suit cards than as trump — a side-suit ace is a free trick, while the same ace inside trump is just another trump. 不要一能亮就亮。发牌早期你只看到自己一小部分牌，第六张就定下的主牌，很可能最后张数很少。另外：A 和 K 当副牌比当主牌更好用——副牌的 A 是白赢一轮，而同一张 A 放在主牌里只是又一张主牌而已。

4. Bury the kitty to create a void4. 扣底的首要目标是缺门

If you win the declaration you pick up the kitty and bury the same number of cards. The single best thing you can do with it is void an entire side suit — once you hold none of a suit, you can ruff it with trump every time it is led, all hand long. 赢得亮主后你会拿到底牌，并扣回同样张数。扣底能做的最有价值的一件事，就是把某一门副牌彻底扣光（缺门）——一旦你这门牌一张不剩，此后每次别人打这门，你都可以用主牌毙掉。

Never bury: 绝对不要扣：

  • Aces. An ace is a trick you can simply take.A。A 是你随手就能赢下的一轮。
  • Trump, jokers, or the rank card. They are your control.主牌、王、级牌。它们是你的控制力。
  • Pairs. The one sanctioned exception is a pair sitting in a short suit you are voiding completely — then the void is worth more than the pair.对子。唯一被认可的例外，是这个对子正好在你要彻底扣光的那门短花色里——此时缺门的价值高于对子。

Points in the kitty are a bet. Whoever wins the last trick takes the kitty at double value, so burying 10s and Ks is profitable only when you expect to win that last trick. Strong hand: bury points. Weak hand: bury none. 往底牌里扣分是一场赌博。谁赢下最后一轮，谁就以双倍拿走底牌，所以只有当你有把握赢下最后一轮时，扣 10、K 才划算。手牌强就扣分，手牌弱就一分不扣。

5. Lead aces, then pairs and tractors5. 先出 A，再出对子和拖拉机

When you have the lead, in order: 当你领出时，优先顺序是：

  1. Side-suit aces and any other single that cannot be beaten. Free tricks that also pull out opponents' high cards.副牌的 A，以及任何已经无人能压的单张。白赢一轮，还能逼出对手的大牌。
  2. Boss pairs and tractors. Lead them early, before the cards that make them get played separately and the structure falls apart.最大的对子和拖拉机。要趁早打出去，否则组成它们的牌被拆开打掉，牌型就散了。
  3. A long side suit you control. Running it drains the opponents' trump.你能控住的长套副牌。一路走下去可以消耗对手的主牌。
Never do this 绝对不要这样做

Do not open a trick with a naked joker or with the rank card. It wins a trick you did not need to win, tells everyone how strong you are, and burns the card that was going to take your last trick. The only exception is a monster trump holding (roughly 15+) where you are deliberately bleeding everyone else dry of trump. 不要用光杆的王或级牌来领出。它赢下的是一轮你本来不需要赢的牌，还暴露了你的实力，更烧掉了本该给你保最后一轮的那张牌。唯一的例外是你手上主牌极多（大约 15 张以上），此时你是在有意把所有人的主牌都抽干。

6. Feed your partner, starve your opponents6. 给队友喂分，绝不给对手送分

When you are not leading, the question is never "what is my best card?" — it is "who is winning this trick?" 当你不是领出方时，要问的从来不是“我最大的牌是哪张”，而是“这一轮现在是谁赢”。

  • Partner is winning → dump points. Throw the 10, the K, the 5. Do not save them — saving points is how they end up in the opponents' pile later. 队友赢着 → 垫分。把 10、K、5 都甩出去。别攒着——攒下来的分，最后往往落进对手手里。
  • Opponent is winning → give nothing. Play a worthless card, and break up a pair if that is what it takes to avoid handing over a point card. 对手赢着 → 一分不给。打无用的牌；如果只有拆对子才能避免送出分牌，那就拆。
  • You cannot win the trick → play your smallest card. Never burn a high trump or a joker on a trick you were never going to take. 这轮你赢不了 → 打你最小的牌。绝不要在一轮本就赢不了的牌上浪费大主牌或王。

7. Remember what has been played7. 记牌

This is the biggest single gap between an average player and a strong one, and it needs no talent — only attention. You do not have to remember all 108 cards. Track the top of each suit and you will know exactly when your own cards become unbeatable. 这是普通玩家与高手之间最大的差距，而且它不需要天赋，只需要专注。你不必记住全部 108 张牌，只要盯住每门花色的顶端，你就能准确知道自己手里的牌什么时候变成了无人能压的牌。

Say you hold 10♡ 10♡ and a K♡, and hearts is not trump. Both A♡s have been played? Your K♡ is now boss. Both A♡s, both K♡s, and the Q♡s and J♡s are gone? Your 10♡ pair is boss — and worth 20 points. 比如你手上有一对 10♡ 和一张 K♡，而红桃不是主。两张 A♡ 都出过了？你的 K♡ 现在就是最大牌。两张 A♡、两张 K♡，以及 Q♡、J♡ 也都走完了？那你这对 10♡ 就是最大对子——而且值 20 分。

8. Plan for the last trick8. 为最后一轮做打算

The last trick pays out the kitty, doubled. That single trick is often worth more than the previous ten combined, and it is decided by what you kept, not by what you played. 最后一轮结算底牌，而且是双倍。这一轮的价值常常超过前面十轮的总和，而它的胜负取决于你留下了什么，而不是你打出了什么。

  • Buried points in the kitty? Save a winner — a joker or a high trump — for the very end.底牌里扣了分？那就留一张能赢的牌（王或大主牌）到最后。
  • Kitty is empty? Stop protecting it. Spend your high cards freely and get your points out early.底牌是空的？那就别再守它。放手用你的大牌，早点把分处理掉。
  • Attacking, and you suspect the declarer buried points? Try to be the one holding the last winner.你是进攻方，并且怀疑庄家扣了分？那就设法让最后那张能赢的牌握在自己手里。

Hand evaluation, with numbers用数字评估手牌

Count the trump in a two-deck pack: 26 cards of the trump suit, 6 off-suit rank cards, and 4 jokers — 36 trump out of 108. You are dealt 25, so your expected trump count is 36 × 25 ÷ 108 ≈ 8.3. That single number anchors every judgement you will make: 数一数两副牌里的主牌：主牌花色 26 张、其他花色的级牌 6 张、王 4 张——共 36 张主牌，总牌数 108。你拿到 25 张，因此主牌张数的期望值是 36 × 25 ÷ 108 ≈ 8.3。这个数字是你一切判断的基准：

12+ Controlling. You can ruff freely, bleed trump, and bury points confidently.掌控局面。可以放心毙牌、抽主，也可以放心往底牌扣分。
8–11 Normal. Play the standard plan; bury a moderate number of points at most.正常。按常规打法走；扣底最多扣中等数量的分。
6–7 Short. Stop planning to win the last trick; get points out early instead.偏少。别再指望赢下最后一轮，改为尽早把分处理掉。
≤5 Weak. Bury nothing valuable and play a pure denial game.很弱。有价值的牌一张都别扣，彻底改打拦截。

Burying the kitty, card by card逐张演示扣底

The bots bury with a strict priority order, and it is worth copying exactly: (1) complete a full side-suit void if one fits; (2) bury the lowest worthless cards from your shortest remaining suit; (3) add points only up to a budget set by your hand strength. Aces, trump, the rank card and pairs are off-limits — the sole exception being a pair that sits inside the suit you are voiding. 机器人扣底遵循严格的优先级，值得照搬：(1) 如果能扣光一整门副牌，就先扣光它；(2) 从剩下最短的花色里扣掉最小的废牌；(3) 只在手牌强度允许的“分数预算”内扣分牌。A、主牌、级牌和对子都不能扣——唯一的例外是这个对子正好落在你要扣光的那门花色里。

Wrong 错误打法 Bury the eight lowest worthless cards — 3♧ 7♧ 8♧ 9♧, 4♡ 8♡, 6♢ 9♢. It looks tidy and safe, and it achieves nothing: you are still long in every suit, so you can never ruff, and you have kept 20 points of K♢s in hand where the opponents can take them off you. 把八张最小的废牌扣掉——3♧ 7♧ 8♧ 9♧、4♡ 8♡、6♢ 9♢。看起来整齐又安全，实际上什么也没做成：你每门花色仍然有牌，所以永远毙不了牌；而那对值 20 分的 K♢ 还留在手里，随时会被对手赢走。
Right 正确打法 Bury the whole diamond suit — K♢ K♢ 9♢ 6♢ — then 4♡, 3♧, 8♡, 7♧. 扣掉整门方块——K♢ K♢ 9♢ 6♢——再加上 4♡、3♧、8♡、7♧。

Why: you are now void in diamonds, so every diamond led for the rest of the hand is a trump trick for you — and with 9 trump you can afford them. The K♢ pair costs 20 points, which is exactly the budget a hand with one joker and nine trump allows, and those points are now safe: they come back to you doubled if you take the last trick, instead of being fought over in the middle game. This is the one sanctioned reason to bury a pair.

The same hand, weaker: swap the big joker for a low club and drop to six trump, and the budget falls to 10 — the K♢ pair no longer fits, so the void is not worth starting. Bury scattered trash instead and play for denial. 理由：你现在方块缺门，此后每次有人打方块，对你来说都是一轮可以毙的牌——而你有 9 张主牌，完全支撑得住。那对 K♢ 花掉 20 分，恰好等于“一张王 + 九张主牌”这类手牌的预算上限；而且这些分现在是安全的：只要你赢下最后一轮，它们会以双倍回到你手上，而不必在中盘反复争夺。这正是唯一被认可的“可以扣对子”的理由。

同样的牌，若变弱：把大王换成一张小梅花、主牌降到六张，预算就只剩 10 分——那对 K♢ 就装不下了，这个缺门也就不值得去做。此时应改为零散地扣废牌，专心打拦截。

Tempo: the low-trump hand-off节奏：小主牌过渡

This is the single most under-used play in the game, and it is the defenders' main mid-game weapon. Once you have cashed your winners and have nothing left worth leading, do not lead a big card. Lead a small, non-point trump — a 3♤ or 4♤. 这是全局中最被低估的一手，也是防守方中盘的主要武器。当你已经把大牌兑现完、手里没有值得领出的牌时，不要打大牌，而要领出一张小的、不带分的主牌——比如 3♤ 或 4♤。

It does three things at once: 它同时做到三件事：

  1. It forces the attackers to spend real trump on a trick worth nothing.迫使进攻方在一轮毫无价值的牌上消耗真正的主牌。
  2. It hands the lead to your partner, who can then run the suit you are void in.把领出权交给队友，队友随后就能走你缺门的那一套。
  3. It costs you almost nothing — you were never winning that trick anyway.对你几乎零成本——那一轮你本来也赢不了。

Passing the lead correctly正确地把牌权交出去

Mid-game. You are defending. Your side-suit aces are spent, and you hold: big joker, 2♤, 9♤, 3♤, Q♡ J♡, 8♧. Your partner still holds a long club suit. 中盘，你是防守方。你的副牌 A 已经用完，手上剩：大王、2♤、9♤、3♤、Q♡ J♡、8♧。你的队友手上还有一长套梅花。

🃏 🂢 🂩 🂣 🂽 🂻 🃘
Wrong 错误打法 Lead the big joker. You win the trick — and then you are still on lead with nothing good to play, you have destroyed your last-trick insurance, and the opponents happily discarded their junk. 领出大王。你确实赢了这一轮——然后领出权还在你手上，而你没有好牌可打；你毁掉了保最后一轮的那张牌，对手则趁机把废牌垫掉了。
Right 正确打法 Lead the 3♤. An attacker must beat it with a genuine trump; your partner then takes the lead soon after and runs clubs — a suit you are nearly out of, so you ruff the round after that. Meanwhile the joker and 2♤ are still in your hand for the finale. 领出 3♤。进攻方必须用真正的主牌来压它；随后队友很快拿到领出权，开始走梅花——而你这门几乎没有牌了，于是再下一轮你就能毙牌。与此同时，大王和 2♤ 依然留在你手里，用来收官。

The rule: hand off with your smallest trump. Never with a joker, never with the rank card, and never with a trump that carries points — those are exactly the cards the hand-off is designed to preserve. 要点：过渡要用你最小的主牌。绝不要用王，绝不要用级牌，也绝不要用带分的主牌——这几张恰恰是这一手要保住的牌。

The long-suit throw长套甩牌

Holding seven of a side suit with no trump support feels like a dead hand. It is not — it is a weapon, if you are patient. Play the suit normally until both opponents are void in it, then throw the whole remaining holding at once. 手握某门副牌七张、又没有主牌支持，感觉像一手废牌。其实不然——只要有耐心，它就是武器。先按常规打这门牌，直到两名对手都缺了这门，然后把剩下的整套一次性甩出去。

To beat a throw, an opponent must beat every component of it individually, and only with trump. A throw containing two pairs therefore has to be answered with two trump pairs from a single player — which almost never happens. 要击败一次甩牌，对手必须逐一压过它的每一个组成部分，而且只能用主牌。因此，一个含有两个对子的甩牌，必须由同一名玩家拿出两个主牌对子才能压住——这几乎不可能发生。

Throwing a long suit甩出长套

Trump is spades. You have the lead. Both A♧s and both K♧s have already been played, and both opponents have shown themselves void in clubs by ruffing an earlier club trick. You still hold six clubs: 主牌是黑桃。你有领出权。两张 A♧ 和两张 K♧ 都已出完，而两名对手在此前的一轮梅花里都用主牌毙过，说明他们梅花已缺门。你手上还有六张梅花：

🃝 🃝 🃛 🃛 🃙 🃗
Right 正确打法 Throw all six. Q♧Q♧ J♧J♧ is a tractor, and the 9♧ and 7♧ ride along with it. To take this trick an opponent needs a trump tractor plus two more trump, in one hand. Your partner, who is also void, dumps every point card they own into it. 六张全甩。Q♧Q♧ J♧J♧ 是一个拖拉机，9♧ 和 7♧ 一起带走。对手要想赢下这一轮，必须一个人同时拿出一个主牌拖拉机外加两张主牌。而同样缺门的队友，会把手里所有分牌都垫进来。
Wrong 错误打法 Throwing the same six cards before the A♧s and K♧s are gone. The throw is invalid the moment any single opponent can beat one component of it in clubs, and you are forced to lead just that beatable component instead — you lose the whole tempo play and show the table your hand for nothing. 在 A♧ 和 K♧ 还没走完时就甩这六张。只要有任何一名对手能用梅花压住其中一个组成部分，这次甩牌就无效，你会被迫改为只领出那个能被压的部分——整个节奏优势全部丢失，还白白暴露了自己的牌。

Timing is everything: a throw is only as good as your card counting. Wait until the suit's tops are gone and the opponents are void, then take the whole pot at once. 时机就是一切：甩牌的质量完全取决于你的记牌。等到这门花色的大牌都走完、并且对手都缺门，再一次性把整个彩池收走。

Reading voids, and using your partner's读缺门，并利用队友的缺门

Every time a player fails to follow suit, that is permanent public information: they are void in it for the rest of the hand. The bots log every one of these, and so should you. Two tactics follow directly: 每当有人跟不上花色，这就是永久的公开信息：他这一局都缺这门。机器人会把每一次都记录下来，你也应该如此。由此直接引出两个战术：

  • Lead your partner's void — with a cheap card, and only while at least one opponent still has to follow. Your partner ruffs and takes the trick; you spent nothing. Leading a high card here wastes a winner on a trick your partner was going to win anyway. 领出队友缺门的那一套——用便宜的牌，而且要在至少还有一名对手必须跟牌时才这么做。队友毙牌赢下这轮，而你什么也没花。这时若打大牌，就是把一张能赢的牌浪费在队友本来就会赢的一轮上。
  • Respect the opponents' voids — your "boss" ace in a suit both opponents are void in is not boss any more. It is just a card waiting to be ruffed, and if it carries points you are gift-wrapping them. 也要尊重对手的缺门——当两名对手都缺某门时，你在那门里的“最大”A 就不再最大了。它只是一张等着被毙的牌；如果它还带分，那等于把分打包送给对方。

Point discipline: when to break a pair分牌纪律：什么时候该拆对子

Pairs are valuable, but not more valuable than 10 points. When you are void in the led suit and can discard anything, the correct discard is the one that gives the winning opponent the fewest points — even if it breaks a pair. 对子有价值，但没有 10 分值钱。当你缺了领出的那门花色、可以随便垫牌时，正确的选择永远是让赢家拿到最少分的那一手——哪怕要拆掉一个对子。

A ten-point discard decision一次值十分的垫牌抉择

An opponent has led a pair of clubs and is winning the trick. You are void in clubs, so you may discard any two cards. Your non-trump holdings are K♡ 4♡ and 9♢ 9♢ 6♢. 对手领出一对梅花并且正赢着这一轮。你梅花缺门，因此可以任意垫两张。你的副牌是 K♡ 4♡ 和 9♢ 9♢ 6♢。

🂾 🂴 🃉 🃉 🃆
Wrong 错误打法 Discard K♡ 4♡ to keep the 9♢ pair intact. You have just handed the opponents 10 points to preserve a pair of nines that will probably never win a trick. 为了保住那对 9♢，垫出 K♡ 4♡。你刚刚为了留住一对大概率永远赢不了牌的 9，白送了对手 10 分。
Right 正确打法 Break the pair: discard 9♢ 6♢. The opponents get nothing, and you keep the K♡ to dump on your own partner later — where it is worth a full 10 points to your side. 拆对子：垫出 9♢ 6♢。对手一分不得，而你留下 K♡，日后垫给自己的队友——这 10 分就完整地记在你们这边。

Flip it around when your partner is winning: then the K♡ is exactly the right discard. Partner-winning and opponent-winning are mirror images — the same cards, opposite answers. 队友赢着时则完全相反：那时 K♡ 正是该垫的牌。队友赢与对手赢是一体两面——同样的牌，答案完全相反。

The endgame: kitty maths收官：底牌的算术

The kitty is paid to whoever wins the last trick, multiplied by twice the size of the largest component of that trick's format. That multiplier is a lever most players never touch: 底牌归赢下最后一轮的一方，并按该轮牌型中最大组成部分张数的两倍计算倍数。多数玩家从来没想过要去动这个杠杆：

Last trick was led as…最后一轮领出的牌型 Kitty multiplier底牌倍数
a single card单张 ×2
a pair对子 ×4
a tractor of two pairs两对连的拖拉机 ×8

So a 20-point kitty is worth 40 to an attacker who wins the last trick with a single — but 160 to one who arranges to win it with a tractor. If you are attacking and you believe the declarer buried heavily, engineer the last trick: keep a trump pair back rather than a lone joker. 因此，一个 20 分的底牌，对用单张赢下最后一轮的进攻方来说值 40 分——而对能用拖拉机赢下最后一轮的人来说值 160 分。如果你是进攻方，并且判断庄家扣了重底，就要刻意去设计最后一轮：留一个主牌对子，而不是留一张光杆的王。

The defender's min-max 防守方的取舍算式

If you buried 10 points, the kitty is worth at least 20 to the other side. That sets your budget precisely: you may concede up to about 20 points elsewhere in order to guarantee you take the last trick. Conceding more than the kitty is worth means you have lost the trade — at that point, stop protecting it and fight for the points on the table instead. 如果你扣了 10 分，那么这个底牌对对方而言至少值 20 分。这就精确地定下了你的预算：为了确保拿下最后一轮，你最多可以在别处让出约 20 分。一旦让出的分超过底牌本身的价值，这笔交易就亏了——那就别再守底，转而去争台面上的分。

Playing against the bots如何对付各难度机器人

All bots except Omniscient are strictly honest — they only ever see their own hand and the public play history, exactly like you. Knowing how each one thinks tells you how to beat it. 除 Omniscient 之外，所有机器人都是严格诚实的——它们只能看到自己的手牌和公开的出牌记录，和你完全一样。了解它们各自的思路，就知道该怎么打败它们。

Tier难度 How it plays / how to beat it打法特点 / 破解思路
Easy Plays the basic heuristic noisily, with occasional deliberate blunders and no card memory. Punish it by running long suits — it will not track what has gone. 按基础启发式打，但带噪声，会偶尔故意失误，且完全不记牌。用长套硬走就能惩罚它——它不会记住哪些牌已经出过。
Expert A neural net trained on the strongest teacher, used as the prior for a short search. Solid and unexciting; beat it with the tempo plays above — the hand-off and the long-suit throw. 以最强教师蒸馏出的神经网络作为先验，再配一段短搜索。稳健但缺乏亮点；用上文的节奏打法——小主牌过渡与长套甩牌——来击败它。
Enoch Plays this guide, essentially. Perfect card memory, strict discipline: it never opens with a joker, always dumps points to a winning partner, hands off with low trump. That consistency is also readable — you can predict its next move, and set traps that its playbook will walk into. 基本上就是照着本指南打。记牌完美、纪律严格：从不用王领出，队友赢着就一定垫分，过渡一定用小主牌。这种一致性同时也意味着可读——你可以预判它的下一手，并布下它的手册一定会踩进去的陷阱。
Grandmaster The strongest honest tier. It uses the playbook only to propose moves, then simulates each one to the end of the hand and plays whatever the simulations rate highest — so it will break the playbook's instincts when the calculation disagrees. Do not expect it to be predictable. 最强的诚实难度。它只用手册来提出候选着法，然后把每一手模拟到本局结束，最终选择模拟评价最高的那一手——因此当计算与直觉冲突时，它会毫不犹豫地违背手册。别指望能预判它。
Omniscient Sees every hand. It is a practice target, not a fair opponent — and it is badged as a cheater in the lobby for exactly that reason. 能看到所有人的手牌。它是练习用的靶子，不是公平的对手——大厅里也正因如此把它明确标注为“作弊”。
Stuck on a play? 拿不准该出什么？

The ✨ button on your turn asks the server for the move the Grandmaster tier would make from your seat. It is computed only from what you are allowed to see, so it never leaks a hidden card — it is a second opinion, not an oracle. 轮到你时出现的 ✨ 按钮，会向服务器询问 Grandmaster 难度在你这个位置会怎么打。它只根据你有权看到的信息计算，绝不会泄露任何暗牌——它是第二意见，而不是天机。

Advanced checklist进阶速查表

  • Count trump before the first trick. Above 12, take control; below 6, play denial.第一轮之前先数主牌。12 张以上就掌控局面，6 张以下就打拦截。
  • Declare on pairs and tractors, not raw length — and never on card six.亮主看对子和拖拉机，而不是单纯的张数——也绝不要在第六张牌就亮。
  • Bury for a void first, points second, and only within your strength budget.扣底先求缺门，其次才是扣分，而且必须在强度预算之内。
  • Cash boss singles, then boss pairs and tractors, early.趁早兑现最大的单张，然后是最大的对子和拖拉机。
  • Hand off with your smallest trump; never with a joker or the rank card.过渡用最小的主牌；绝不用王或级牌。
  • Throw a long suit only once its tops are gone and the opponents are void.只有当这门花色的大牌走完、且对手缺门时，才甩长套。
  • Break a pair rather than concede a point card to a winning opponent.宁可拆对子，也不要把分牌送给赢着的对手。
  • Keep a trump pair, not a lone joker, if you intend to multiply the kitty.若打算放大底牌倍数，要留一个主牌对子，而不是一张光杆王。
